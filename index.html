What Is Real Estate?

Real Estate = Land + Permanent Improvements

Land

The natural surface of earth including soil, water, and natural resources.

Buildings

Structures permanently attached to land such as houses, towers, malls and factories.

Infrastructure

Roads, bridges, utilities and urban systems supporting civilization and economic activity.

Land vs Real Estate vs Real Property

Term Meaning
Land Natural surface of earth
Real Estate Land + buildings/improvements
Real Property Real estate + legal ownership rights

Main Types of Real Estate

Residential

Houses, apartments, villas, condominiums and living spaces.

Commercial

Office towers, hotels, shopping malls and retail business properties.

Industrial

Factories, logistics hubs, warehouses and production facilities.

Agricultural

Crop farms, fisheries, dairy farms and agricultural land systems.

Special Purpose

Schools, airports, mosques, hospitals and stadiums.

Real Estate Industry Structure

Developers

Build and develop projects.

Investors

Invest capital for long-term profit.

Banks

Provide financing and mortgages.

Architects

Design buildings and urban systems.

Government

Create regulations and zoning policies.

Property Managers

Maintain and manage completed assets.

Development Process

1. Land Acquisition

Purchase land for development.

2. Feasibility Study

Analyze demand, profit and legal conditions.

3. Design & Planning

Create layouts, architecture and utility systems.

4. Financing

Arrange investment and capital structures.

5. Construction

Build the physical structure and infrastructure.

6. Marketing & Sales

Sell or rent the completed project.

Real Estate Economics

Price ∝ Demand / Supply

Location

The most important factor in determining value and demand.

Interest Rates

Lower interest rates increase buyer demand.

Population Growth

More people create higher housing demand.

Investment Models

Buy & Hold

Buy property and wait for appreciation over time.

Rental Income

Generate monthly cash flow from tenants.

Flipping

Renovate undervalued property and resell for profit.

Real Estate = Finance + Construction + Law + Economics + Human Psychology
